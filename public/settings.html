Settings & privacy

Privacy

Show my online status When this is off, nobody sees you as "Online". You can still see who else is online.

Blocked users

Blocked people can't message or call you, can't invite you to rooms, and never see when you're online. They are not told they were blocked.

Notifications

Show message text in notifications On: the alert shows who wrote and what they said. Off: just "New message". The text is added on your phone after decrypting - it never goes through Google's servers. If the app has been fully closed, the alert can only show who wrote (the message is still encrypted until you open the app).

App lock & screen privacy

PIN lock Ask for a PIN when you open the app or come back to it. After 10 wrong tries the app signs out and erases the messages stored on this device.
Lock afterTime in the background before the PIN is needed again.
Unlock with fingerprintThe PIN always works as a backup.
Block screenshots & hide app preview Stops screenshots and screen recording, and shows a blank tile in the recent-apps list.

Account

Signed in on this device

Messages you've read are kept in an encrypted vault on this device (that's what lets the server forget them). This button wipes that vault and this device's one-time keys.
